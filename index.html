Why Amber & Oak

Three things we never compromise on

Beans, roasted weekly

We roast in small batches every week, so what's in your cup was never sitting in a warehouse.

Baked fresh, every morning

Our pastry case is restocked before sunrise by our in-house baker — nothing trucked in frozen.

A place to actually sit

Big tables, good light, and no pressure to rush out the door once your cup is empty.
