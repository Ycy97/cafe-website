Our story

Coffee, slowed down.

How three friends and one very old espresso machine became Amber & Oak.

Since 2019

It started with a broken espresso machine

Amber & Oak began in 2019, when three friends pooled their savings to buy a decommissioned espresso machine from a closing restaurant. None of us had run a cafe before — we just knew we wanted a place where the coffee was taken seriously and the people weren't.

Since then, we've roasted well over forty tonnes of coffee, worn out two espresso machines (properly this time), and gotten to know most of our regulars by their order before they reach the counter. The name comes from our two favourite things in the shop: the amber glow of a good espresso shot, and the oak counter our first machine sat on.
